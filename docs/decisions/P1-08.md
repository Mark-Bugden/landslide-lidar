## D04 · `in_mapped` sheet union

- **Decision.** `in_mapped` is True when the polygon's `representative_point()` (GEOS PointOnSurface, on the geometry
  as delivered) lies `within` the union of all 860 surveyed sheets, full and partial, reprojected with
  `landslide.sheets.load_sheets` (D03).
- **Why.** BRIEF.md note 2 and PLAN.md D04 default.
- **Alternatives.** The union of the 843 full sheets only gives 18,606 [E, exploration]. A boundary-inclusive test
  (`covered_by`) and points taken from repaired geometries both give 18,649 [E], so neither choice matters here.
- **Evidence.** [E] 18,649 of 21,356; brief and spike 18,649.
- **Deviation?** No.

## D05 · Polygon area and eligibility

- **Decision.** `area_m2` is the planar area of the delivered geometry in EPSG:5514. Eligible = `skupina ==
  labels.skupina` (1), `in_mapped == labels.in_mapped` (True), `area_m2 >= labels.min_area_m2` (3,000).
- **Why.** BRIEF.md note 2; PLAN.md D05 default.
- **Alternatives.** The register's own `st_area(shape)` field gives 15,083: three polygons are on different sides of
  3,000 m² (objectids 6894, 10088, 18220, net +1). The field differs from the geometry area by a median of −0.15 m²
  and at most 721 m². Area from repaired geometries also gives 15,082.
- **Evidence.** [E] 15,082; brief and spike 15,082.
- **Deviation?** No.

## D05a · Invalid register polygons (new)

- **Decision.** 68 of the 21,356 register polygons are invalid (self-touching or self-intersecting rings). They keep
  their delivered geometry for the area (D05), the representative point (D04) and in `polygons.parquet`. Only the
  overlays (intersection areas and the `intersects` test of the sheet kraj, polygon → sheet) use a repaired copy:
  `shapely.make_valid(method="structure", keep_collapsed=False)`, applied to invalid geometries only.
- **Why.** GEOS overlays can fail on invalid input; the counts above don't depend on the repair.
- **Evidence.** [E] GEOS reasons: 62 "Ring Self-intersection" (a ring touching itself), 6 "Self-intersection". The
  repair changes their areas by at most 0.27 m².
- **Deviation?** No.

## kraj1 (part of D06)

- **Decision.** `kraj1` = the first comma-separated token of `gen_kraj`, stripped ("Jihomoravský, Zlínský" →
  "Jihomoravský"). Every polygon has a `gen_kraj` [E].
- **Why.** Appendix A §4 "first-listed `gen_kraj`"; PLAN.md D06.

## D07 · Flysch outline ("2 km morphological closing")

- **Decision.** Union of the geology polygons whose `subregion` is in `flysch.subregions` (all 597 in the file), then
  `buffer(+2000).buffer(−2000)` (the config's `closing_m` is the buffer distance; round joins, 8 segments per quarter
  circle), then every interior hole filled, then parts under 1 km² dropped. The outline is the layer
  `flysch_outline` of `data/derived/flysch_outline.gpkg`; the other reading is stored as `flysch_outline_alt` but is
  not used. `flysch_frac` = area(sheet ∩ outline) / area(sheet); a sheet is flysch if `flysch_frac >=
  flysch.min_sheet_frac` (0.5).
- **Why.** Appendix A §3; PLAN.md D07 default.
- **Alternatives.** A 2 km structuring element read as its diameter, i.e. `buffer(+1000).buffer(−1000)`:
  6,525.3 km² and 299 flysch sheets [E]. The only sheet whose flag differs between the readings is 25-13-15 (flysch
  under the default reading only).
- **Evidence.** [E] Union 6,441.4 km² in 8 parts with 14 holes; after the closing 6,585.6 km² in 2 parts with no
  holes, so the hole filling and the 1 km² rule change nothing under the default reading (under the other reading
  they fill 2 holes). 300 flysch sheets. Spike 6,586 km² and 300. The default was the plan's D07 default and was set
  before the areas were computed; §5 agrees with it and not with the other reading, but was not used to choose.
- **Deviation?** No. The 1 km² threshold and the hole filling come from Appendix A §3 text; they have no config key
  (module constants `FLYSCH_FILL_HOLES`, `FLYSCH_MIN_PART_M2`).

## D-new-1 · DTM grid and validity per sheet

- **Decision.** The 860 package rasters are read one at a time (whole band, read-only). The grid columns
  (`grid_xmin`, `grid_ymax`, `grid_w`, `grid_h`) come from each raster's own transform and size, not from the
  recipe. `has_zero_cells`: some cell is exactly 0 m. `dtm_all_valid`: the config's
  `splits.stage2.require_valid_dtm` ("no 0-m cells or NaN"), read with the full `dtm.invalid_rule`, i.e. no NaN or
  non-finite cell, no nodata cell, no z ≤ 0 and no z > 2000 (both thresholds parsed from the rule text). `dtm_path`
  is relative to the raw-data directory (`Paths().raw`), e.g. `dmr5g/14_34_07.tif`.
- **Why.** P1-03 D03 recommends reading the grid from the raster. The stage-2 criterion names 0 m cells and NaN; the
  invalid rule also covers negative and > 2000 m cells, which a "valid" raster shouldn't have either.
- **Evidence.** [E] 96 sheets have 0 m cells (248,483,932 cells in all); 764 are fully valid; no sheet has a NaN,
  negative or > 2000 m cell (maximum 1,426.16 m), so the two readings of "valid" coincide. The raster grid equals
  the recipe grid for 860 of 860 sheets.
- **Deviation?** No. `geo.invalid_bounds` repeats `landslide.dtm.invalid_bounds` (P1-04), which was merged into
  `phase-1` after this branch was cut; it should be replaced by the P1-04 function after the merge.

## D-new-2 · Polygon → sheet

- **Decision.** Each eligible polygon goes to the sheet with the largest area of intersection with it (all 860
  sheets are candidates, stage-2 sheets included). Exact ties go to the lowest sheet number; a polygon with no
  positive overlap gets none. `polygons.parquet` holds `sheet` (and `sheet_share`, the share of the polygon inside
  it) for eligible polygons only. `n_eligible` on a sheet counts the eligible polygons assigned to it.
- **Why.** Appendix A §4 ("the single sheet that holds the largest part of its area"); PLAN.md §4.3.
- **Evidence.** [E] All 15,082 eligible polygons are assigned; 1,460 straddle a sheet edge or the mapped area's
  edge, 34 have less than half their area in their sheet (smallest share 0.29). 771 sheets hold at least one eligible
  polygon.
- **Deviation?** No.

## D06 · Sheet kraj

- **Decision.** For each sheet, the `kraj1` of every register polygon (all 21,356, any `skupina`, `in_mapped` or not)
  that intersects the sheet (`intersects`, so touching counts) gets one vote. The kraj with most votes wins. Ties go
  to the larger summed intersection area, then to Python string order. A sheet with no intersecting polygon has no
  kraj, and then no transfer group and no stratum.
- **Why.** Appendix A §4; PLAN.md D06 default, fixed before the data were seen.
- **Alternatives.** Appendix A gives no tie rule. Two other rules were checked after the build, as a counterfactual
  only:
  - lowest objectid (the kraj whose polygon comes first in the register, as `Counter.most_common` or
    `value_counts().idxmax()` would give over polygons in file order): all four strata equal §5 exactly
    (242/34/8,000, 42/18/575, 39/18/372, 428/96/5,670) [E];
  - plain alphabetical order: N_ZLMS and N_other equal §5, F_ZLMS and F_other don't [E].

  So the spike most likely broke ties by first occurrence [I]. I kept the D06 default rather than switch to the rule
  that reproduces §5, because choosing it now would be fitting a split to the spike's numbers (PLAN.md §5: "never
  tune sampling or splits to hit them"). This is an open question for the orchestrator: the change is one function
  (`geo.majority_kraj`), and it moves 2 sheets with 2 eligible polygons each.
- **Evidence.** [E] 793 sheets by majority, 4 by the area tie rule, none needed the alphabetical step; 63 sheets (56
  full) have no intersecting polygon and none of them has an eligible polygon. The four tie sheets:

  | Sheet | Votes | Ours (area) | Lowest objectid | Effect on the splits |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 11-42-04 | Karlovarský 1, Plzeňský 1 | Karlovarský | Plzeňský | none (N_other either way) |
  | 25-12-13 | Moravskoslezský 1, Olomoucký 1 | Olomoucký | Moravskoslezský | N_other here, N_ZLMS in the spike (2 eligible) |
  | 25-13-15 | Olomoucký 2, Zlínský 2 | Olomoucký | Zlínský | F_other here, F_ZLMS in the spike (2 eligible) |
  | 35-11-25 | Jihomoravský 31, Zlínský 31 | Jihomoravský | Jihomoravský | none |

  35-11-25 holds 52 eligible polygons; §5's F_ZLMS count rules out Zlínský there, which the area rule and the
  lowest-objectid rule both give [I].
- **Deviation?** No; recorded as a difference from §5.

## Transfer group and stratum

- **Decision.** `group_b` = "train" if the sheet's kraj is in `splits.transfer_train_kraje` (Zlínský,
  Moravskoslezský), else "test". `stratum` = flysch flag × group: F_ZLMS, F_other, N_ZLMS, N_other. Both are set for
  every sheet that has a kraj (797), including stage-2 sheets and sheets without eligible polygons, as sheet
  attributes (Appendix A §4 lists them under "Sheet attributes"); the split lists use stage-1 sheets only.
- **Deviation?** No.

## D08 · 15 km blocks

- **Decision.** The block cell of a sheet is (`floor(cx / 15000)`, `floor(cy / 15000)`) of its polygon centroid in
  EPSG:5514 (`splits.block_km` = 15), from the EPSG:5514 origin. A block is (stratum, cell), so blocks nest in
  strata. Blocks are assigned to stage-1 sheets only. The id is opaque: `<stratum>-<k>`, with k numbering the
  stratum's blocks by their smallest member sheet number (e.g. `F_ZLMS-007`). Ids carry no coordinates; the cell
  indices stay in `sheets.parquet` (`block_cx`, `block_cy`). The same id is used in every file.
- **Why.** Appendix A §4; PLAN.md D08 (opaque ids in committed files). Numbering by member sheet keeps the ids stable
  and free of anything derived from sheet geometry.
- **Evidence.** [E] 165 stage-1 blocks, 1–13 sheets each; strata 33 / 18 / 18 / 96 blocks (table above).
- **Deviation?** No.

## Stage-1 sheets

- **Decision.** `is_stage1` = not a stage-2 sheet and `n_eligible >= 1`. Partial sheets count (9 stage-1 sheets are
  partial), as do sheets with 0 m cells (77); P1-09's patch rules handle both.
- **Evidence.** [E] 751 sheets, 14,617 eligible polygons; spike 751 and 14,617.

## D09 · (a) fold assignment

- **Decision.** The flysch stage-1 blocks (F_ZLMS and F_other) are sorted by id, permuted with
  `default_rng(11).permutation`, stable-sorted by eligible-polygon count (descending) and assigned one by one to the
  fold with the fewest eligible polygons so far, ties to the lowest fold index. `splits.n_folds_a` = 5.
- **Why.** Appendix A §4 ("shuffled (seed 11), sorted by eligible-polygon count, assigned greedily"); PLAN.md D09.
  D02: the method names the seed, so the generator is `default_rng(11)`.
- **Evidence.** [E] Folds of 10, 10, 10, 11 and 10 blocks with 1,715, 1,715, 1,715, 1,716 and 1,714 eligible
  polygons (47–64 sheets); spike 10–11 blocks and 1,714–1,716.
- **Deviation?** No. The seed 11 has no `splits` key; it equals `sampling.seed`, but the code uses the module
  constant `splits.FOLD_SEED` so that the folds don't change if the sampling seed ever did.

## D10 · Inner validation

- **Decision.** For each run, the training blocks are: a_f*k*, the flysch blocks not in fold *k*; b, the blocks of
  the ZLMS strata (group "train"); s2, all stage-1 blocks. They are sorted by id, permuted with
  `default_rng(seed)` (1000 + *k*, 2000, 3000) and added in that order until they hold at least
  `splits.inner_val_frac` (0.15) of the run's training eligible polygons. The comparison is exact (0.15 as 3/20), so
  float rounding can't ask for an extra block.
- **Why.** Appendix A §4; PLAN.md D10. The config's s2 text says "15% of blocks as inner validation"; D10 applies the
  §4 polygon rule to s2 as well, so every run uses one rule.
- **Evidence.** [E]

  | Run | Seed | Training blocks | Training eligible | Inner-val blocks | Inner-val eligible | Share |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | a_f0 | 1000 | 41 | 6,860 | 7 | 1,121 | 0.163 |
  | a_f1 | 1001 | 41 | 6,860 | 9 | 1,127 | 0.164 |
  | a_f2 | 1002 | 41 | 6,860 | 12 | 1,445 | 0.211 |
  | a_f3 | 1003 | 40 | 6,859 | 5 | 1,086 | 0.158 |
  | a_f4 | 1004 | 41 | 6,861 | 7 | 1,269 | 0.185 |
  | b | 2000 | 51 | 8,368 | 7 | 1,500 | 0.179 |
  | s2 | 3000 | 165 | 14,617 | 17 | 2,211 | 0.151 |

  The overshoot comes from the last block added (a_f2's last block takes it from below 0.15 to 0.21).
- **Deviation?** No.

## D-new-3 · Values with no config key

- **Decision.** Module constants, each citing its source: `splits.STAGE2_SHEETS` (Appendix A §4), `splits.FOLD_SEED`
  = 11, `splits.INNER_VAL_SEED_A_BASE` = 1000, `INNER_VAL_SEED_B` = 2000, `INNER_VAL_SEED_S2` = 3000 (Appendix A §4),
  `geo.FLYSCH_FILL_HOLES` = True and `geo.FLYSCH_MIN_PART_M2` = 1,000,000 (Appendix A §3), and our own
  `geo.BUFFER_QUAD_SEGS` = 8 (shapely's default buffer resolution).
- **Why.** `configs/landslide.json` has no keys for them, and P1-08 does not own the config.
- **Alternatives.** Config keys, proposed for P1-16: `replication.splits.stage2_sheets` ({flysch, nonflysch}),
  `replication.splits.fold_seed`, `replication.splits.inner_val_seeds` ({a_base, b, s2}),
  `replication.flysch.fill_holes`, `replication.flysch.min_part_km2`, `replication.flysch.buffer_quad_segs`.

## Licence and committed outputs

- Sheet geometry, the outline, flysch fractions, centroids, block cells, grid coordinates and the per-sheet kraj
  votes stay in `data/derived/` (D06 quotes the vote counts of the four tie sheets: counts of register polygons only). The tracked `results/sheet_splits.csv` has exactly: sheet, kraj, flysch, stratum, block (opaque
  id), fold_a, group_b, is_stage1, is_stage2. `results/splits_summary.json` holds aggregate counts and areas, the
  stage-2 checks (flags and eligible counts) and library versions; it carries no coordinates or per-sheet fractions.
  No figures.
